### Recitals
A. The Parties informally discussed collaborating on a mobile application currently known as `[___ nombre de la app]` (the "**App**").
B. Contributor's involvement was limited to opening and/or paying for developer/store accounts (Apple App Store and Google Play) and related setup costs not exceeding approximately USD `[___ importe, p. ej. 500]` (the "**Contribution**").
C. No company, corporation, partnership, joint venture or other legal entity was ever formed between the Parties, and the App has not been published.
D. The Parties now wish to fully and finally settle and terminate any and all relationship between them concerning the App, on the terms below.

### 1. Settlement Payment
Owner shall pay Contributor a total of USD `[___ importe total: gastos + extra]` (the "**Settlement Payment**"), as full and final reimbursement of the Contribution and in consideration of the releases and assignments herein. Contributor accepts the Settlement Payment as **full and final settlement** of any and all amounts, rights or claims of any kind.

### 2. Assignment of Rights and Accounts
Contributor hereby irrevocably assigns and transfers to Owner all right, title and interest of any kind in and to the App and anything related to it, including any developer/store accounts, materials, names, domains, designs, code, data and intellectual property (and waives any moral rights therein, to the extent permitted by law). Contributor agrees to **close, transfer or relinquish** any Apple or Google developer/store account he created in connection with the App and to **not publish, distribute or exploit** the App in any form.

### 3. Full and Mutual Release
Effective upon receipt of the Settlement Payment, each Party irrevocably **releases and forever discharges** the other from any and all claims, demands, debts, rights, causes of action and liabilities of any kind, whether known or unknown, arising out of or relating to the App or the Parties' dealings. Contributor specifically and expressly **waives any claim to equity, shares, ownership, partnership status, profits, revenue, royalties or future participation** of any kind in the App or in any company that Owner may form relating to it.

### 4. No Partnership
The Parties acknowledge and agree that **no partnership, joint venture, agency, company or other legal entity was ever created** between them, and that nothing in their prior dealings shall be construed as having created one. Neither Party has authority to bind the other.

### 5. Cooperation
Contributor shall promptly sign any reasonable document and take any reasonable action requested by Owner to give effect to the transfer/closure of accounts and the assignments in this Agreement, at no further cost.

### 6. Confidentiality and Non-Disparagement
Each Party agrees to keep the terms of this Agreement confidential and to refrain from making disparaging statements about the other or about the App.

### 8. Entire Agreement
